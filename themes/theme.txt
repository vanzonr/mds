This setting selects the overall beamer theme of the slides.
#

# Boadilla, CambridgeUS, Copenhagen, Darmstadt, Frankfurt, Ilmenau,

# Bergen, Berkeley, Berlin, boxes, default, Dresden, Goettingen,
# Hannover, Luebeck, Malmoe, Marburg, Montpellier, Pittsburgh,
# Rochester, Singapore, and Szeged
#
